# Text Sentiment Annotation Project

## 1. Project Overview
This is a practical demonstration of text sentiment annotation. The objective is to classify customer feedback based on the sentiment expressed in each statement.

## 2. Annotation Labels

- **POSITIVE:** Expresses satisfaction, happiness or approval.
- **NEGATIVE:** Expresses dissatisfaction, frustration or disappointment.
- **NEUTRAL:** Contains no clear positive or negative opinion.
- **MIXED:** Contains both positive and negative opinions.

## 3. Annotation Guidelines

1. Read the entire customer statement before assigning a label.
2. Identify the overall sentiment and context.
3. Assign only one appropriate label.
4. Avoid making assumptions beyond the text.
5. Review classifications for consistency and accuracy.

## 4. Sample Annotated Dataset

| Customer Feedback | Label | Reason |
|---|---|---|
| The service was excellent and delivery was fast. | POSITIVE | Expresses satisfaction. |
| I am disappointed with the poor customer service. | NEGATIVE | Expresses dissatisfaction. |
| My order arrived yesterday. | NEUTRAL | States a fact without emotion. |
| The product is beautiful, but delivery was very late. | MIXED | Contains positive and negative opinions. |
| I love the quality of this product. | POSITIVE | Expresses approval. |
| The package arrived damaged. | NEGATIVE | Expresses a negative experience. |

## 5. Quality Control

- Checked each statement against the annotation guidelines.
- Ensured labels matched the overall sentiment.
- Reviewed ambiguous statements carefully.
- Maintained consistency across classifications.

## 6. Project Outcome

This exercise demonstrates text classification, sentiment analysis, guideline interpretation, attention to detail and annotation quality control.
